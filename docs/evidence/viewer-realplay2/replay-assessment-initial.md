The other three fallback publications also say things are normal/fine despite kritik's supplied stressed state. Fallback is part of the visible quality result, not evidence that the model answered successfully.
- **Unsupported personal details passed validation:** snapshot seed 2 sequence 10 changes to calm weather; snapshot seed 3 sequence 6 claims a work scene; snapshot seed 3 sequence 10 changes knitting to a spinning wheel; fresh seed 1 sequence 35 invents hot tea and non-itchy wool. Some are harmless soft details, but they were not supplied as authorized direct-answer content.

Fresh seed 3 sequence 10 demands a description of electrical-wire voltage. The associated plan was `Mood` with clear mood/current-activity facts.
- **The model sometimes gives related but unclear Russian:** “ранки всё ломят”, “рандомные матчи меняют весь день”, “моё настроение как у жирафа с блоком”, and several garbled knitting phrases. Not every terse or playful reply is wrong, but these cannot be credited as clear, meaningful answers simply because the validator accepts them.
- The exam story was correctly rejected against kritik's actual day, and an over-strong profanity response was correctly rejected. The replacement text nevertheless flattened the supplied stressed mood into “fine”.

These are visible quality failures after the structured target/purpose/context changes. Preserve the current selected models, report this evidence separately, and do not claim the next human gate has passed. No implementation or model changes were made as part of this assessment.
