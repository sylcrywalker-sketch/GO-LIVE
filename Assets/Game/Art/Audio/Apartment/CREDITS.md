## Required attribution — radiator clips

**RadiatorTick_01.wav** and **RadiatorTick_02.wav** are adapted from “radiator (constant clicking sound)” by **Nanisia**.
Source: https://freesound.org/people/Nanisia/sounds/493229/

License: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Modifications: isolated two 160 ms metal-click excerpts; decoded the public HQ MP3 preview, downmixed to mono, resampled to 48 kHz, high-pass filtered at 650 Hz and low-pass filtered at 6200 Hz, attenuated continuous steam hiss with a local RMS-keyed soft gate and release envelope, removed DC offset, applied fades and gain adjustment, and saved PCM16 WAV with TPDF dither. The original creator does not endorse this adaptation.

Retain this attribution and the license link when distributing either radiator clip or an adaptation of it.

## Source quality and preservation

Freesound inputs are publicly available full-duration HQ MP3 previews, not the original WAV/FLAC uploads. Prepared WAVs add no further lossy encoding. Kenney footstep OGGs are copied without change. The prepared WAVs are not claimed to restore information absent from the source MP3s.

The saved source pages and official CC license texts were retained outside Assets in Library/ApartmentAudioSources. No private/user audio was accessed.
